Why VS exists

Too many people walk into this alone.

When someone reports harassment or assault in uniform, the accused often has a lawyer. The person who reported often doesn't. Cases stall. People get moved while the perpetrator stays. Help through official channels can be confusing, slow, and discouraging enough that many stop asking.

VS was started by service members who deployed together and lived some of this firsthand. We're building the place we wish we'd had: plain-language resources, independent professionals who understand military cases, and a community that reminds you that you are not the only one.

Did you know?

Help you may not know you have.

Counseling without reprisal

Seeing a free, outside counselor doesn't get reported to your command. Military OneSource offers confidential non-medical counseling sessions at no cost.

MST and VA care

Military sexual trauma can be connected to a VA claim, and the VA provides MST-related care — even if you didn't report at the time.

Outside counsel matters

An independent, civilian attorney can advocate for you in ways the system won't do on its own.

Eligibility rules change — always confirm details directly with the provider.
