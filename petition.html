Clear policy, everywhere you serve.

Service members assigned to joint and NATO commands can fall into gaps where there is no clear harassment policy of their own — leaving them to whatever the host component's policy happens to be. We think everyone in uniform deserves clear protection and a clear path to report, wherever they're stationed.

What we'll ask for

Clear, consistent harassment and assault policy for joint and multinational commands, and real protection from reprisal for those who report.
