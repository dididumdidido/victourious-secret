Hearing that someone else made it through can be the thing that keeps you going. Soon this page will hold stories — told anonymously or by name — from people who are finding their way forward.

Before you share, know the risks

If you're still serving, going public can affect your career, your unit, and any open case. Talk to a lawyer before sharing details of an active investigation. Sharing anonymously is always an option, and we will never publish identifying details without your clear permission.

Our story guidelines

Share your experience, not someone else's name. No identifying details about others. Focus on what helped — the small victories. We review every story before it's posted, and you can ask us to take yours down at any time.
