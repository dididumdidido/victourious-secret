About VS

Victourious Secret.

VS exists to help people affected by harassment, sexual harassment, or assault in the military take back their power and find small victories on the road to healing.

Why the name

Too many people carry what happened to them as a secret — alone, without a lawyer, without a community. VS turns that around. The victory doesn't have to be one big win; it's the small ones. Making a call. Finding a counselor. Telling one person. Every one of them counts.

What we do — and don't

We do: point you to independent lawyers, counselors, and practitioners; gather anonymous data to show the scale of the problem; build community; and push for better policy.

We don't: give legal or medical advice, replace the services the DoD or VA already provide, or ever share your information without your permission.

Who we are

We're service members and veterans who deployed together. We've seen firsthand how hard it is to get help when you need it most. We lead as stewards — every person on the team brings their own skills, and every voice shapes what VS becomes.
